FAQ

Questions we get every week.

What exactly does Cosa CRM replace?

For most teams: the CRM, funnel/landing page builder, email marketing platform, SMS tool, scheduler, call tracking, form builder, e-signature tool, review software and course platform. If a tool in your stack isn't covered, we'll tell you straight on the call rather than pretend otherwise.

Can you migrate our existing contacts and pipelines?

Yes. We import contacts, custom fields, tags, notes and open opportunities, and rebuild your pipeline stages to match how you already work — so your team doesn't have to relearn their day on top of learning a new tool.

How long does setup take?

A standard build is live within a few business days. Larger migrations with multiple locations, sub-accounts or heavy custom automation typically run one to two weeks. You'll get a firm timeline before anything starts.

Do we need technical skills to run it?

No. We do the build and the configuration, then train your team on the daily workflow — check the pipeline, work the inbox, book the appointment. The automation runs underneath without anyone touching it.

Why isn't pricing listed on the site?

Because the honest number depends on seats, message volume and how much of the build you want us to do. Rather than publish a tier you'd have to reverse-engineer, we scope it live on the call and you leave with a real figure.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. Cancel any time — and your data is yours, exportable on the way out.

What kind of support do we get?

A direct line to a real person who knows your account, plus a shared channel for day-to-day questions. You're not filing tickets into a queue.
